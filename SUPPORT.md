# Getting help

- **Questions and ideas**: ask in [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/Lets-Decode-AI/uttr/discussions).
- **Bugs**: open an [issue](https://github.com/Lets-Decode-AI/uttr/issues/new/choose) with your OS, uttr version, model and reproduction steps.
- **Feature or model requests**: open an issue with the matching template.
- **Security problems**: do not post them publicly. Follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Search existing issues and discussions first.
